# Kathleen Fisher

## Biography

Kathleen Fisher has been instrumental in bringing CHERI from research into national programmes. A long‑standing leader in secure systems and programming languages, she has championed CHERI through major DARPA initiatives. She has now joined [ARIA](/organisations/aria/) as a CEO to continue connecting deep technical foundations with real‑world impact and deployment.
