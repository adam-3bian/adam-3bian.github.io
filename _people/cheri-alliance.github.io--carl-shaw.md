## Biography
{: id="biography" }

Carl Shaw is a hands-on technical expert with a diverse background in embedded systems and security engineering.

His work focuses on the intersection of hardware and software, with a particular interest in software-defined hardware architectures for security and safety. Carl brings deep expertise in areas such as secure software development, firmware and OS security, side-channel and fault injection attack mitigation, and threat modelling.
