# Merve Gülmez

## Biography

Merve Gülmez is a researcher at [Ericsson](/organisations/ericsson/) Security Research. She holds a PhD in Computer Science from [KU Leuven](/organisations/ku-leuven/), and her research focuses on systems security, memory safety, and improving software resilience.
