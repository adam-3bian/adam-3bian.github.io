## Biography

Alex Rebert is an engineer at Google, where he focuses on memory safety. Previously, he co-founded ForAllSecure and led the creation of Mayhem, the autonomous cyber reasoning system that won the 2016 DARPA Cyber Grand Challenge. He was recognized by MIT Technology Review as one of the 35 Innovators Under 35 and by Forbes’s 30 Under 30.
