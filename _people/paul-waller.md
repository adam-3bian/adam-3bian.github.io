# Paul Waller

Board role: Head of Capability Research

## Biography

Paul Waller works on advancing practical, deployable cyber security at national scale. At the [NCSC](/organisations/ncsc/), he focuses on how technologies like CHERI and memory safety translate into real risk reduction and adoption across critical systems.
