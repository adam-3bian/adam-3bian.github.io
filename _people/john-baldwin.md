# John Baldwin

## Biography

John Baldwin has been an active [FreeBSD](/software/freebsd/) developer for over 25 years working on a broad range of subsystems in the kernel and bits of userspace. John has also contributed to GDB. John began working with the CHERI project for over 10 years including work in [CheriBSD](/software/cheribsd/) and CHERI GDB and more recently dabbling in CHERI [LLVM](/software/llvm-and-clang/) and [Morello](/hardware/morello/) LLVM.
