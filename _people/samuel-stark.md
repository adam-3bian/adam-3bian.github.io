# Samuel Stark

## Biography

Samuel W. Stark is a final-year PhD student at the [University of Cambridge](/organisations/university-of-cambridge/), studying alternate capabilty systems under Simon W. Moore, with a published article on the topic in Communications of the ACM in collaboration with Simon W. Moore and [A. Theodore Markettos](/people/a-theodore-markettos/).
