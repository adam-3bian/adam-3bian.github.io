## Biography
{: id="biography" }

Samuel W. Stark is a final-year PhD student at the University of Cambridge, studying alternate capability systems under Simon W. Moore, with a published article on the topic in Communications of the ACM in collaboration with Simon W. Moore and A. Theodore Markettos.
