# Manu Ravishankar

## Biography

Manu Ravishankar is a Principal Consultant specialising in innovation strategy, programme design and technology commercialisation. Formerly Head of Insights and Impact for Innovate UK’s £450m Strategic Innovation Fund, he led funding strategy, oversaw whole-system project portfolios and regulatory engagement.

He has shaped national innovation programmes for DESNZ, [DSIT](/organisations/dsit/) and Ofgem, and previously led energy‑system innovation work at the Carbon Trust. His expertise spans emerging technology assessment, market and policy analysis, and designing high‑impact innovation pathways.
