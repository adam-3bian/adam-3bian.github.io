## Biography

Adam Finney is a CHERI technologist with over 20 years of systems experience, and a key driver of CHERI adoption. As Lead of the Adoption Programme and Marketing Working Group, he focuses on turning security into something developers and businesses can actually use. Adam has worked at the sharp end of the stack—from porting Linux to Morello to shaping next‑generation CHERI‑enabled FPGA platforms and developer boards.
