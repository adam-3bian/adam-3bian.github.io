# David Chisnall

Board role: Director of Systems Architecture

## Biography

David Chisnall has been working on CHERI since 2012, when he joined the [University of Cambridge](/organisations/university-of-cambridge/) to lead the languages and compiler work on the CHERI project. He joined Microsoft in 2018, where he adapted several in-house ISAs to CHERI, worked on high-performance server-class CHERI implementations, and also created and led the [CHERIoT](/hardware/cheriot/) project, which Microsoft open sourced in 2023. He is a co-founder of [SCI Semiconductor](/organisations/sci-semiconductor/), which is building CHERIoT microcontrollers. Along with [Yucong Tao](/people/yucong-tao/) (Microsoft) and [Ben Laurie](/people/ben-laurie/) ([Google](/organisations/google/)), he is a co-maintainer of the CHERIoT platform.
