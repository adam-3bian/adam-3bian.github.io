## Biography
{: id="biography" }

Dave Harcourt is the CISO for BT International and a BT Fellow, with decades of experience shaping cyber security at scale. He sets security strategy for BT and influences direction across industry, driven by a clear principle: make secure‑by‑default the easiest path to follow. His focus is on security that actually gets adopted.
