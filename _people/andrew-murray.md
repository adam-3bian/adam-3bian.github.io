# Andrew Murray

## Biography

Andrew Murray is founder and director of [The Good Penguin](/organisations/the-good-penguin/), a UK based consultancy with a focus and passion for embedded Linux, low level and open-source software. The Good Penguin has contributed to the ISCF Digital Security by Design programme, developing and maintaining ARM’s official [Morello](/hardware/morello/) Yocto distribution. Before founding The Good Penguin, Andrew established Embedded Bits, an embedded Linux consultancy, which is now the UK presence for Witekio.

Andrew has previously worked at Arm within the Open Source Software team working as a Linux kernel developer. There, he delivered upstream support for ARMv8-A architectural features which included support for PMU, KVM, coresight, atomics and profiling.
