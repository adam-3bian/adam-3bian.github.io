# Jianyi Cheng

## Biography

Jianyi Cheng is an Assistant Professor in the School of Informatics at the [University of Edinburgh](/organisations/university-of-edinburgh/). His research aims to automatically produce small, fast, low-energy, and secure computing systems using formal methods. His research interests include computer architecture, systems, compilers, and formal methods.

Prior to this, he was a Research Associate in the [University of Cambridge](/organisations/university-of-cambridge/) Computer Laboratory with Prof. Tim Jones. He completed his PhD in the CAS group at Imperial College London under the supervision of Prof. George Constantinides and Dr. John Wickerson.
