# Phil Day

## Biography

Phil is the Director of Engineering at [Configured Things](/organisations/configured-things/), a startup founded by Alumni from Hewlett Packard Labs to build solutions that can operate resiliently across security domains. He has been a regular speaker at forums such as the IoT Security Foundation conference and CyberCon, and is a contributor to the [CHERIoT](/hardware/cheriot/) ecosystem and a technical reviewer for the CHERIoT programmers’ Guide. He has more years experience than he cares to admit to developing and delivering complex distributed systems.
