# Paul Metzger

## Biography

Dr Paul Metzger is a Research Associate in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at the [University of Cambridge](/organisations/university-of-cambridge/). He recently started working on CHERI OS API portability and has been involved with CHERI projects since 2021. Prior to that, he received his PhD from the [University of Edinburgh](/organisations/university-of-edinburgh/).
