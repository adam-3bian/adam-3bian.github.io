# Javier Orensanz Martinez

## Biography

Javier is a business leader with over 25 years of experience in the semiconductors industry. He was the general manager of Arm’s tools business unit for 8 years, and is currently CEO of [lowRISC](/organisations/lowrisc/). He holds a degree in telecommunication engineering and an MBA.
