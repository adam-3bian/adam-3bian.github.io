## Biography

Mike Eftimakis has an extensive background in the semiconductor and electronics industry with 30 years in senior technical and business roles. He has a rich history of innovation with companies like VLSI Technology, NewLogic, Arm and [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/), and he started-up and led his own company. where he played pivotal roles in advancing technology and business strategies. His expertise ranges from chip design engineering and system architecture to product and company management, marketing and strategy, making him a key contributor to the growth and success of microelectronics organisations.

In parallel to his VP Strategy and Ecosystem role at Codasip, he is the Founding Director of the [CHERI Alliance](/organisations/cheri-alliance/). He is now managing the Alliance on a day-to-day basis, and is leading the Promotion and Ambassador Program and the Certification working groups.
