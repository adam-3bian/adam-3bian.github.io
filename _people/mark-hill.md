# Mark Hill

## Biography

Dr Mark B. Hill, BEng PhD CEng MIET

Mark is Lead CPU Architect at [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/) and has 30 years industrial experience in processor and processor subsystem design and development at plus a BEng(Hons) First and a PhD from Southampton University. He has been involved in the design and development of several RISC-V cores for over the last 7 years for use in a wide range of industrial and consumer products and been a strong advocate of CHERI for several years.
