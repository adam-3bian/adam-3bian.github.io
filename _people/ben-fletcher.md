## Biography
{: id="biography" }

Ben Fletcher has been involved in SoC development for over 25 years. He has worked in design, verification, software, and more recently Architecture. He has worked for Codasip for 2 years, and is currently working on performance models and architectures of out-of-order cores, along with SoC developments (all featuring CHERI technology).
