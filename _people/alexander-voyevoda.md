# Alexander Voyevoda

## Biography

Alex Voyevoda is a senior systems engineer focused on turning high‑assurance ideas into production systems. With over 30 years of experience in low‑level C systems on Linux, he leads the implementation of Unicus Edge on [Morello](/hardware/morello/) CHERI at [UM Labs](/organisations/um-labs/) — bringing capability‑based memory safety and post‑quantum cryptography into real‑world streaming and IoT deployments.

Alex holds a Ph.D. in Computer Aided Design from the National Technical University of Ukraine, combining academic research with long-term industrial systems development.
