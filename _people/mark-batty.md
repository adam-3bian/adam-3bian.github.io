# Mark Batty

## Biography

Mark Batty has played a key role in the effort to building foundational specifications for mainstream systems, focusing on concurrency in the pervasive C and C++ languages.

He is a Professor at the [University of Kent](/organisations/university-of-kent/) and lead of the Programming Languages and Systems group there. He has been funded by the EPSRC, the Royal Academy of Engineering (RAEng), GCHQ, VeTSS, and was a Principal Investigator in the first and second round of [UKRI](/organisations/ukri/) DSbD projects.
