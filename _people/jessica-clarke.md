# Jessica Clarke

## Biography

Jessica Clarke is a Senior Research Software Engineer at the University of Cambridge, contributing to the maintenance and development of CHERI LLVM and CheriBSD, and has been involved with the project since 2016 when she wrote the first prototype of a CHERI linker.

She takes a particular interest in the low-level parts of toolchains and runtimes, including linkers and loaders, and has been vice-chair of the RISC-V psABI Task Group since its inception in 2021.
