## Biography
{: id="biography" }

Donato Ferraro is a Ph.D. student in Computer and Data Science for Technological and Social Innovation at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia.

He works with Minerva Systems, and his research focuses on security, safety, real-time, and embedded systems. His current work investigates the application of CHERI technology in drone environments.
