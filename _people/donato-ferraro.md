# Donato Ferraro

## Biography

Donato Ferraro is a Ph.D. student in Computer and Data Science for Technological and Social Innovation at the [University of Modena and Reggio Emilia](/organisations/university-of-modena-and-reggio-emilia/).

He works with [Minerva Systems](/organisations/minerva-systems/), and his research focuses on security, safety, real-time, and embedded systems. His current work investigates the application of CHERI technology in drone environments.
