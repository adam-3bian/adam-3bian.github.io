# Robert Watson

## Biography

Professor Robert N. M. Watson is a Professor of Systems, Security, and Architecture at the [University of Cambridge](/organisations/university-of-cambridge/)’s Department of Computer Science and Technology. His research encompasses computer architecture, compilers, program analysis, operating systems, networking, and security. He leads several cross-layer research projects in these areas.

Professor Watson is a director of the [CHERI Alliance](/organisations/cheri-alliance/), board member of the [FreeBSD Foundation](/organisations/freebsd-foundation/) and a co-author of The Design and Implementation of the [FreeBSD](/software/freebsd/) Operating System. He has contributed extensively to the FreeBSD Project and has a strong interest in open-source software.
