## Biography
{: id="biography" }

Prof. John Goodacre: University of Manchester, Director Digital Security by Design: UKRI

John is Professor of Computer Architectures at the Department of Computer Science, The University of Manchester in the UK having previously spent 17 years with Arm Ltd as the Director of Technology and Systems. He is also appointed by UK government’s Research &amp; Innovation agency as the Director of the Digital Security by Design, a £200M programme to enable industry and researchers to create a step change in approach to cybersecurity – by design.
