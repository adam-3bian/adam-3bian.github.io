# Edoardo Marangoni

## Biography

Edoardo has been working with compilers for the majority of his professional career. After working in the Wasm space, he joined SCI Semiconductor and started working on this project since its beginning. He has been involved with every aspect of it, ranging from technical tasks such as adding the CHERIoT target to organising work for the team and communicating about the project to make it more visible.
