# Edoardo Marangoni

## Biography

Edoardo has been working with compilers for the majority of his professional career. After working in the Wasm space, he joined [SCI Semiconductor](/organisations/sci-semiconductor/) and started working on this project since its beginning. He has been involved with every aspect of it, ranging from technical tasks such as adding the [CHERIoT](/hardware/cheriot/) target to organising work for the team and communicating about the project to make it more visible.
