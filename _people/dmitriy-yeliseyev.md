## Biography

Dmitriy is an embedded systems software architect and technical expert with over 25 years of experience. Their career began in telecommunications, developing embedded software for network systems. Over time, they transitioned into industrial automation and later specialized in safety-critical industries such as medical devices, aerospace, and defense. Since 2010, They have been with Wind River GmbH, focusing on system architecture, advanced technology research, and compliance with industry standards. Previously, they contributed to software architecture and embedded solutions at Hermes SoftLab and worked on telecommunication infrastructure at IskraTEL. They hold a Master’s degree in Computing and Intelligent Systems &amp; Networks from the National University of Radio Electronics in Kharkiv, Ukraine.
