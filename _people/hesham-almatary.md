## Biography
{: id="biography" }

Hesham’s research interests involve security, operating systems, systems software, and computer architecture. They got their PhD from the University of Cambridge on CHERI compartmentalisation for embedded systems.

Beyond research, they have been working with and contributing to open-source projects, including: Linux, seL4, RTEMS, and FreeRTOS.
