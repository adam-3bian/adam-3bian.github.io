## Biography

Domagoj Stolfa is a researcher currently working at Capabilities Limited and University of Cambridge. He’s done work on kernels, hypervisors, tracing systems and language runtimes. He implemented initial libunwind support for CheriBSD’s c18n and has since been working on V8 and its integration into the Chromium web browser.
