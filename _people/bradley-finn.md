# Bradley Finn

## Biography

Bradley Finn is Head of Cyber Innovation Policy at the UK’s Department for Science, Innovation and Technology. He is responsible for polices and programmes to support the growth of the cyber sector across the UK and help innovators develop world leading cyber tech.
