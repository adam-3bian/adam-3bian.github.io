# Andy Moore

## Biography

Andy Moore drives awareness and adoption of RISC‑V across its global member ecosystem. As Director of Marketing at RISC‑V International, and with deep prior experience at Arm, he brings a rare mix of processor IP marketing and hands‑on engineering.
