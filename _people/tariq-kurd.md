# Tariq Kurd

## Biography

Tariq Kurd has been involved in CPU architecture, design, and verification for over 25 years, primarily in the embedded field. He has worked on VLIW, multithreading, out-of-order cores, security, and DSP style cores. He has been with [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/) for over three years and previously worked at Huawei UK, Broadcom and Nvidia.

Tariq previously chaired [RISC-V International](/organisations/risc-v-international/)’s Zfinx and Code Size Reduction task groups. He started working looking at CHERI around 2021 and is the lead architect of Codasip’s X730 which is the world’s first commerically available CHERI-RISC-V application processor.
