# Jeremy Singer

## Biography

Jeremy is a Reader in Programming Language Implementation at the [University of Glasgow](/organisations/university-of-glasgow/). He is the proud owner (or at least, custodian) of four [Arm Morello](/hardware/morello/) boxes and two [lowRISC](/organisations/lowrisc/) [Sonata](/hardware/sonata/) boards.
