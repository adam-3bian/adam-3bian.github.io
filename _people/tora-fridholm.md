## Biography

Tora Fridholm is an experienced B2B marketing professional with a focus on transforming complex technology into clear, value-driven messaging. Currently, she leads marketing and communications at Codasip, a processor technology company, where she drives messaging, launches, and media communications. Previously, Tora served as Chief Marketing Officer at IAR Systems, overseeing global marketing strategies and leading cross-functional teams. With a Master of Arts in Computational Linguistics from Uppsala University, Tora combines her technical background with a passion for innovation to align marketing efforts with company strategy and build impactful partnerships.
