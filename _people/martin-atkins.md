## Biography

Martin is a software consultant with over 35 years of experience in research, and commercial development projects in the UK and abroad. Technical areas have included safety-critical systems (air-traffic control and Radar), real-time embedded operating systems, and web applications.

Martin specialises in rapid prototyping in Python, test harnesses, safety-critical development, and open-source, and also has experience of team leading, technical writing, network management, and teaching.
