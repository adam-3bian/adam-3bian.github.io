## Before you send

The contact form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number and your message so the co-operative can respond to your enquiry.

The housing application asks about you, your household and your housing needs so that your application can be considered. National Insurance numbers, health details and equal opportunities monitoring are optional in this online version. Include information about another person only with their knowledge and authority.

## Sending your information

When online sending is available, completed forms are sent to the co-operative's configured email inbox through its separate form service. The website does not keep a separate application database. The receiving email system retains the message so the co-operative can deal with it. Short-lived technical records help limit spam and duplicate submissions; they do not contain your form answers.

The form uses [ALTCHA](https://altcha.org/), an open-source spam-protection check hosted with the form service. It requires JavaScript and does not use tracking cookies or image puzzles. If you cannot use the check, telephone the office or [download the paper application]({{ '/assets/documents/housing-application-form-2025.pdf' | relative_url }}).

Your answers remain on the current page while you work. The form does not save a draft in your browser. Closing or reloading the page can lose your answers. Use **Review answers** to check a housing application before sending it.

If a form says online sending is unavailable, checking your answers does not send them. Please telephone the office or use the paper application instead. A successful submission means the mail server accepted the message; it does not mean an offer of housing.

Do not attach identity documents, payment details or medical records to a general enquiry. The office will explain how to provide supporting evidence if needed.
