## Sharing an opportunity
{: id="sharing-an-opportunity" }

Alliance members can use the [contact page](/alliance/membership/contact/) to propose a CHERI-related role for inclusion. A useful submission includes the employer, location, working pattern, role level, closing date, original vacancy link, and a short explanation of its connection to CHERI.
