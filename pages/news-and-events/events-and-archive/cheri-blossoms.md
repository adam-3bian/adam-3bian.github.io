# CHERI Blossoms Conference

As the CHERI community’s annual gathering, the conference brings together researchers, engineers, product teams, policymakers, educators, and adopters to share progress, exchange ideas, and help shape the future of secure computing.

Organised by the CHERI Alliance and set in the birthplace of CHERI, it connects research, implementation, standards, policy, and real-world deployment through talks, tutorials, demonstrations, and discussions spanning the entire ecosystem.

Whether you’re exploring CHERI for the first time or already helping drive adoption, you’ll gain practical insight, technical knowledge, and direct access to the people and organisations advancing the field.

## What to expect

### Understanding the bigger picture

Discover why memory safety has become a global priority, how CHERI is addressing some of the industry’s most important security challenges, and where adoption is gaining momentum across industry, government, and academia.

### Deep technical insight

Hear directly from the researchers and engineers advancing the state of the art in hardware, software, verification, compartmentalisation, performance, and real-world deployment.

### Hands-on learning

Explore CHERI-enabled systems through tutorials and live demonstrations. Access slides, recordings, and practical resources that help you continue your learning long after the event.

### Meet the CHERI community

Connect with the people building, deploying, researching, and standardising CHERI technologies. Whether you’re evaluating CHERI, contributing to its development, or planning adoption, CHERI Blossoms offers a unique opportunity to build relationships across the ecosystem.
