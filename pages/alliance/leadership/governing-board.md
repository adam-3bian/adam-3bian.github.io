# Governing Board
{: id="page-hero-title" .page-hero-title }

The Governing Board provides organisational stewardship with representation from commercial, research, and public-interest security organisations.
{: .page-hero-summary }

The board stewards the Alliance’s mission, priorities, and responsible operation. Its range of perspectives helps connect technical development with product delivery, open ecosystems, research, infrastructure, and public cybersecurity needs.

## Current board
{: id="directory-current-board" }

The Governing Board consists of individuals elected by our members for two-year terms, with half of the seats renewed each year, together with one representative from the CHERI Alliance C.I.C. Board of Directors.
