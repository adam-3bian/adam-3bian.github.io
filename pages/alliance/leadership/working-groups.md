## Overview

Working groups bring together experts from across the CHERI ecosystem to share knowledge, solve common challenges, advance technology and standards, and support real-world deployment.

Many of the most important conversations in the CHERI ecosystem happen within working groups. Members can **learn** from peers, **influence** future directions, and stay close to **emerging developments**. For many organisations, working groups are a key reason to join the Alliance.


## Participation

Working groups provide a forum to discuss requirements, share implementation experience, review proposals, coordinate projects, and develop practical guidance.

Some activities are member-only, while many repositories, publications, and technical outputs are developed within working groups before being released publicly.

## Finding the right group

Working groups evolve as the ecosystem grows, and new groups may be created to address emerging needs.

Visit the [contact page](/alliance/membership/contact/) if you would like help finding the most relevant group or learning how to participate.
