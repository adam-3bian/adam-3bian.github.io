# Reports &amp; White Papers
{: id="page-hero-title" .page-hero-title }

## Reports and white papers
{: id="directory-reports-and-white-papers" }

These reports provide broader introductions, programme evidence, adoption research, and policy context. They share the same `_data/papers.yml` source as the academic bibliography and are identified with `white_paper: true`.

1. Report or white paper
   {: .resource-list__type }

   ### [Digital Security by Design Programme Outcomes](https://www.ukri.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/IUK-030625-DigitalSecurityDesignProgrammeOutcomes.pdf)

   Innovate UK. Programme outcomes report, 2025.
   {: .resource-list__citation }

   Records the outcomes and evidence developed through the Digital Security by Design programme.
2. Report or white paper
   {: .resource-list__type }

   ### [CHERI Technology for Cyber Security](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cheri-technology-for-cyber-security)

   Department for Science, Innovation and Technology. UK Government research report, 2025.
   {: .resource-list__citation }

   Summarises CHERI's potential contribution to reducing cyber risk and improving the resilience of digital systems.
3. Report or white paper
   {: .resource-list__type }

   ### [CHERI Adoption and Diffusion Research](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cheri-adoption-and-diffusion-research)

   Department for Science, Innovation and Technology. UK Government research report, 2024.
   {: .resource-list__citation }

   Examines the potential market for CHERI and factors that may shape adoption and diffusion.
4. Report or white paper
   {: .resource-list__type }

   ### [Assessing the Viability of an Open-Source CHERI Desktop Software Ecosystem](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/security/ctsrd/pdfs/20210917-capltd-cheri-desktop-report-version1-FINAL.pdf)

   Robert N. M. Watson, Ben Laurie, and Alex Richardson. Capabilities Limited technical report, 2021.
   {: .resource-list__citation }

   Assesses the work required to adapt a substantial open-source desktop software stack to CHERI.
5. Report or white paper
   {: .resource-list__type }

   ### [An Introduction to CHERI](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/techreports/UCAM-CL-TR-941.pdf)

   Robert N. M. Watson, Simon W. Moore, Peter Sewell, and Peter G. Neumann. University of Cambridge Technical Report UCAM-CL-TR-941, 2019.
   {: .resource-list__citation }

   A high-level introduction to the CHERI architecture, microarchitecture, formal models, and software work.
