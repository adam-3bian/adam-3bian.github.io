## Responsible, Sustainable, Appreciated

Have a Morello board you’re not using? We’d love to help it find a new home where it can make a real difference to accelerate CHERI adoption. Returning it is quick, easy, and appreciated.

## Why Return It?

Morello boards are still very useful for experimenting with CHERI, but also for research and development purposes. A number of people need these boards and come to the Alliance to [request them](/products/morello/). By sending your Morello board back, you’re helping another developer, researcher, or project get off the ground—while also reducing electronic waste.

… and also, you’ll make space in your lab for new things! How about other [CHERI hardware](/build/platforms/cheri-products/)?

## How to Start a Return

Just send us a message and we will discuss the return details.  
We keep the process smooth so you can ship it back quickly. Once we receive the board, we refurbish and reassign it to someone who needs it more.  
Thanks for helping us support the community and keep great CHERI hardware in circulation!
