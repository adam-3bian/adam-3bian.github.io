## Event Overview
{: id="event-overview" }

This full-day hands-on tutorial introduced students, researchers and software developers to CHERI and CHERIoT through practical exercises, live demonstrations and guided experimentation with real hardware and software platforms. It was co-organised by the [CHERI Research Centre](https://cheri.cst.cam.ac.uk/) at [University of Cambridge](/organisations/university-of-cambridge/), [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/), and [lowRISC](/organisations/lowrisc/) alongside the [CHERI Blossoms 2026 conference](/events/cheri-blossoms-conference-2026/).

Participants explored memory safety, compartmentalisation, CHERI-enabled operating systems, and the CHERIoT ecosystem through presentations, code examples and guided exercises. The tutorial provided a practical starting point for anyone interested in experimenting with CHERI technologies.

## Agenda
{: id="agenda" }

The agenda of the tutorial was:

| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:30 – 10:30 | Part I | Introduction to CHERI, memory safety and compartmentalization (1h) |
| 10:45 – 11:45 | Part I | CHERI software experimentation on CheriBSD/Morello (1h) |
| 11:45 – 12:45 | Light lunch with sandwiches (1h) |  |
| 12:45 – 13:45 | Part II | CHERI-RISC-V, Codasip X730 and CHERI Linux (1h) |
| 14:00 – 15:00 | Part II | CHERI-RISC-V, Codasip X730 and CHERI Linux (1h) |
| 15:15 – 16:15 | Part III | CHERIoT, lowRISC’s Sonata FPGA and CHERIoT RTOS (1h) |
| 16:30 – 17:30 | Part III | CHERIoT, lowRISC’s Sonata FPGA and CHERIoT RTOS (1h) |

The tutorial was a perfect opportunity to get to know what the CHERI technology is, what research areas are related to it, and how to get started experimenting with CHERI-extended hardware-software stacks. Participants were expected to read the “[CHERI: Hardware-Enabled C/C++ Memory Protection at Scale](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/security/ctsrd/pdfs/20240419-ieeesp-cheri-memory-safety.pdf)” article published in the IEEE Security &amp; Privacy magazine.

## Venue
{: id="venue" }

The Hands-on Introduction to CHERI and CHERIoT was held in Cambridge UK, at the Department of Computer Science and Technology in the [William Gates Building](https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/directions), on the University of Cambridge’s West Cambridge site, located off Madingley Road.
