## Event Overview

This full-day hands-on tutorial introduced students, researchers and software developers to CHERI and CHERIoT through presentations, code examples, guided exercises and practical experimentation with CHERI-enabled hardware and software platforms.

Held alongside the [CHERI Blossoms 2026 conference](/events/cheri-blossoms-conference-2026/), the tutorial was co-organised by the [CHERI Research Centre](https://cheri.cst.cam.ac.uk/) at [University of Cambridge](/organisations/university-of-cambridge/), [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/), and [lowRISC](/organisations/lowrisc/).

Participants explored memory safety, compartmentalisation and different parts of the CHERI hardware-software ecosystem, progressing from the fundamentals of CHERI to practical experimentation with CheriBSD/Morello, CHERI-RISC-V and CHERI Linux, and CHERIoT. The tutorial provided a practical starting point for anyone interested in understanding CHERI and beginning to experiment with the technology.

## What participants explored

The tutorial provided a practical starting point to get to know what the CHERI technology is, what research areas are related to it, and how to get started experimenting with CHERI-extended hardware-software stacks.

### CHERI fundamentals

An introduction to CHERI, memory safety and compartmentalisation, followed by hands-on software experimentation using CheriBSD and Morello.

### CHERI-RISC-V and Linux

Practical exploration of CHERI-RISC-V, the Codasip X730 and CHERI Linux, connecting the CHERI architecture with an application-class processor and operating system environment.

### CHERIoT

An introduction to CHERIoT, lowRISC's Sonata FPGA platform and the CHERIoT RTOS, exploring CHERI concepts in an embedded environment.

## Continue learning

For background on the concepts explored during the tutorial, participants were recommended to read “[CHERI: Hardware-Enabled C/C++ Memory Protection at Scale](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/security/ctsrd/pdfs/20240419-ieeesp-cheri-memory-safety.pdf)”, published in the IEEE Security &amp; Privacy magazine.

## Venue

The Hands-on Introduction to CHERI and CHERIoT was held in Cambridge UK, at the Department of Computer Science and Technology in the [William Gates Building](https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/directions), on the University of Cambridge’s West Cambridge site, located off Madingley Road.
