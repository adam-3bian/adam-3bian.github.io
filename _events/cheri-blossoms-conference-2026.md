## Three days of CHERI in Cambridge

CHERI Blossoms 2026 brought together the growing CHERI ecosystem to share progress, exchange ideas and explore how CHERI is moving from research towards real-world adoption.

The programme spanned the strategic case for CHERI, industry adoption, regulation and standardisation, through to hardware, software, operating systems, verification, performance and the latest research. Speakers came from organisations including **NCSC**, **ARIA**, **Google**, the **European Commission**, **DSIT**, **UKRI**, **BT**, **RISC-V International**, **Codasip**, **lowRISC** and the **University of Cambridge**.

Alongside the talks, attendees could meet across the exhibition and networking spaces, explore CHERI work from across the ecosystem, and celebrate **15 years of the CHERI project**. For those unable to attend in person, the event was livestreamed on Zoom.

## From strategy to silicon
{: id="from-strategy-to-silicon" }

CHERI Blossoms brought the different parts of the CHERI ecosystem together, from organisations exploring why and how to adopt CHERI to the researchers and engineers building the technology.

### Day 1: CHERI adoption and the emerging ecosystem

The first conference day looked at the bigger picture: the strategic value of CHERI, memory safety at scale, the Cyber Resilience Act, government initiatives, commercial adoption, and progress towards ETSI and RISC-V standardisation.

Speakers included representatives from NCSC, ARIA, Google, the European Commission, DSIT, UKRI, BT, PA Consulting, RISC-V International and the CHERI Alliance, alongside organisations developing and researching CHERI technology.

The day concluded with networking, a light dinner and a celebration of CHERI's 15th birthday.

### Day 2: Building with CHERI

The second day went deeper into the technology, with sessions covering CHERI-enabled hardware, temporal safety, formal verification, performance, operating systems, Rust, compartmentalisation and ongoing CHERI research.

Talks demonstrated the breadth of work now taking place across CHERI, from processor and SoC design through to system software and applications.

### Hands-on with CHERI and CHERIoT

![CHERI tutorial showing a demonstration in a classroom](/assets/events/cheri-blossoms-conference-2026/aff155aa7b69-cheri-tutorial-showing-a-demonstration-in-a-classroom.jpg){: .image-left }

The conference was preceded by a full-day practical [tutorial](/events/cheri-blossoms-tutorial-2026/) organised by the [CHERI Research Centre](https://cheri.cst.cam.ac.uk/), [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/), and [lowRISC](/organisations/lowrisc/).

Designed for students, researchers and developers, the tutorial introduced CHERI and CHERIoT, memory safety and compartmentalisation, before giving participants practical exposure to CheriBSD/Morello, CHERI-RISC-V and CHERI Linux, and CHERIoT RTOS through presentations, examples and guided exercises.
