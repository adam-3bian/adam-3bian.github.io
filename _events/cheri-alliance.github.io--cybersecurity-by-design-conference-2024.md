## About
{: id="about" }

On the **November 12, 2024**, the **Hub8 MX in Cheltenham** hosted a groundbreaking event that bridged the gap between **cutting-edge cybersecurity research** and real-world industry implementation. Hosted by **NCSC, Cynam, and the CHERI Alliance**, this **free-to-attend** conference provided an opportunity to dive deep into the **CHERI architecture**—a revolutionary approach to securing future digital systems.

Through insightful **keynotes** and discussions from leaders from **NCSC**, **DSbD**, **University of Cambridge**, and **CHERI Alliance**, attendees explored the intersection of **government initiatives** and **industry collaboration**. Topics ranged from the semi-technical introduction of **CHERI** to industry-specific strategies for driving **security innovation**.

There were thought-provoking speeches, **exhibitions** showcasing **open-source** and **collaborative projects**, and a **networking reception** to close the day.
