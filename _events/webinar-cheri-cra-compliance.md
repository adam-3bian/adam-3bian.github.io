## Description
{: id="description" }

From December 2027 the EU will enforce the [Cyber Resilience Act](/news/the-eu-cyber-resilience-act-what-it-means-for-secure-by-design-systems-and-cheri/) which forces companies to take security extremely seriously for every device sold in the EU with a “digital element”. This talk is about the details of the regulations why this is expected to dramatically increase the demand for CHERI. The talk will be followed by time for questions and open discussion.

Hosted by [Tariq Kurd](/people/tariq-kurd/) – [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/)
