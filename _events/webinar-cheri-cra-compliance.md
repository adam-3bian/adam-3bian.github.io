## Overview

Why the industry needs CHERI to be able to meet the EU Cyber Resilience Act

## Description

From December 2027 the EU will enforce the [Cyber Resilience Act](/news/the-eu-cyber-resilience-act-what-it-means-for-secure-by-design-systems-and-cheri/) which forces companies to take security extremely seriously for every device sold in the EU with a “digital element”. This talk was about the details of the regulations why this is expected to dramatically increase the demand for CHERI. The talk was followed by time for questions and open discussion.

Hosted by [Tariq Kurd](/people/tariq-kurd/) – [Codasip](/organisations/codasip/)
