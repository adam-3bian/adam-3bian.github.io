# CHERITech'25 Conference

A technical conference in Manchester for researchers, engineers, cybersecurity specialists, and product developers working across the CHERI stack.

Format
: Conference

Series
: CHERITech

Time
: 09:00 to 17:00 GMT
{: .record-properties }

Thank you to all those who attended!

## Conference Overview

CHERITech’25 brought together researchers, technologists, cybersecurity experts, and product developers for a dynamic exploration of Digital Security by Design.

Now under the stewardship of the [CHERI Alliance](/organisations/cheri-alliance/), this year’s conference continued its role as a global hub for innovation in CHERI (Capability Hardware Enhanced RISC Instructions) technology. Attendees engaged with industry leaders, shared insights with a vibrant technical community, and explored cutting-edge implementations shaping the future of secure computing.
