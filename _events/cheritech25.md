## Conference Overview
{: id="conference-overview" }

**CHERITech’25** brought together researchers, technologists, cybersecurity experts, and product developers for a dynamic exploration of Digital Security by Design.

Now under the stewardship of the CHERI Alliance, this year’s conference continued its role as a global hub for innovation in CHERI (Capability Hardware Enhanced RISC Instructions) technology. Attendees engaged with industry leaders, shared insights with a vibrant technical community, and explored cutting-edge implementations shaping the future of secure computing.
