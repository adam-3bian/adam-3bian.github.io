# The Capable Hub

The Capable Hub is a non-profit engineering organisation improving the availability, quality and upstream alignment of open-source CHERI software.

## Description

The Capable Hub is a non-profit engineering organisation improving the availability, quality and upstream alignment of open-source CHERI software.

## CHERI Activities

The Capable Hub provides infrastructure and tooling for CHERI ecosystem, including AWS backed CI, artifact hosting and community mailing lists. To support automated testing, we are currently developing a hardware board farm.

We provide engineering resources to the ecosystem, these efforts are guided by our Technical Advisory Group, which serves as the primary mechanism for the community to steer our technical priorities and resources.

Our initial work includes mapping the ecosystem to centralise available artifacts and the creation of technical guides for those new to CHERI development.
