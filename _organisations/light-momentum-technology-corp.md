## Description

Light Momentum Technology Corp. (LMTech), founded in 2021 by top university alumni in Taiwan, specializes in IC design, AI, and edge computing. LMTech provides integrated services in FinTech, automotive, security, and AI applications, excelling in technical solutions and meeting diverse customer needs. LMTech develops custom chips, enhancing power efficiency, performance, and cost-effectiveness. Through innovative hardware-software integration, LMTech fuses software functionalities with hardware implementations, boosting system performance and stability, reducing power consumption, and enhancing overall system performance and security. The company aims to deliver innovative, efficient, and reliable solutions.

## CHERI Activities

Light Momentum Technology Corp. (LMT) is integrating CHERI technology into its computing solutions and IC products to enhance security and reliability. This strategic move allows LMT to offer cutting-edge solutions that are resilient to emerging threats. As an ambassador for the CHERI alliance in Taiwan and the broader APAC region, LMT is promoting CHERI technology within Taiwan’s tech ecosystem, engaging with local industry stakeholders, including ICT, HPC, and semiconductor companies, to foster a vibrant CHERI community. Furthermore, LMT is collaborating with the agencies and associations to advocate for the adoption of CHERI technology in critical infrastructure, highlighting its robust security features to safeguard essential services and public interests. To accelerate the development of CHERI-based products, LMT is partnering with leading silicon IP providers to co-develop high-performance, secure, and energy-efficient SoC platforms with companion IPs around CHERI-enabled processor cores. This collaboration leverages the IP providers’ expertise in processor architecture, software tools, and verification methodologies. LMT is committed to promoting CHERI technology across the industry, encouraging its adoption in various applications. By leveraging CHERI, LMT aims to deliver innovative solutions that address the evolving needs of its customers and build a more secure and reliable computing future.
