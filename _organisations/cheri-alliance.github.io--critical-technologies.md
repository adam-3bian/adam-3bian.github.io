## Description
{: id="description" }

The Critical Technologies Inc. (CTI) mission is trustworthy networked autonomy. CTI’s near term goal is to return meaningful control of devices, networks and data to their proper owners and enable a safe exit from the Red Queen’s race (of perpetually patching vulnerabilities due to undisciplined development). Our science fiction inspired long term optimistic vision is to lead a safe entry into a strong general AI/ML enabled Gaia protecting personal freedoms for all potential sophonts. CTI has diverse R&amp;D experience, from one of the first broadband cable modems for a commercial vendor in 1993, a massively parallel hardware real time neural network for DARPA in 1995, and resilient tactically deployed IP networks, to explainable AI/ML today.

## CHERI Activities
{: id="cheri-activities" }

CTI began promoting CHERI as part of a trustworthy whole-system stack at the 2019 RISC-V Summit and is working to integrate CHERI in open platforms for trustworthy networked autonomy.
