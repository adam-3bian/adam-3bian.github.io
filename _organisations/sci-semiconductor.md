## Description
{: id="description" }

SCI Semiconductor was formed to lead the commercialisation of CHERI technologies and is a founder member of the CHERI Alliance. With a strong focus on secure and high-integrity computing, the organization has built a team of recognised industry leaders, with decades of leadership in security, processor IP and chip design, and high-integrity software. Through their ICENI device family the team is focused on development of high integrity microcontrollers based on the Microsoft CHERIoT Ibex core.

## CHERI Activities
{: id="cheri-activities" }

SCI Semiconductor is delivering commercial CHERIoT RV32E RISC-V based devices for microcontroller and embedded microprocessor applications. Their ICENI family is targeting a range of high-integrity and high-availability applications encompassing critical infrastructure, aerospace and defence, telecoms, medical and automotive applications. SCI is the co-owner of the CHERIoT core repository alongside Microsoft.
