## Description

University of Cambridge is one of the top ten universities in the world. University of Cambridge and SRI International started working on CHERI in 2010 and have broadened the collaboration to many industry and academic partners as this novel security technology is deployed.

## CHERI Activities
{: id="cheri-activities" }

University of Cambridge and SRI International started working on CHERI in 2010 and have broadened the collaboration to many industry and academic partners as this novel security technology is deployed. Research has led to over 12 Ph.D. dissertations and many peer-reviewed papers. Key personnel have progressed to partner organisation, transitioning the technology and know-how.
