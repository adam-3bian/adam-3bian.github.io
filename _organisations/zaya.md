## Description

We offer security solutions and containerisation for microcontrollers and embedded systems, addressing specific security needs for small and compact devices in markets such as IoT, Secure Elements, and Automotive. Positioned at the cutting edge of IoT device security, ZAYA is the most secure and lightweight operating system on the market. Adopting a unique modular approach, it redefines security standards. From secure isolation and encrypted communications to containerised applications and services, ZAYA provides unmatched security against potential threats, ensuring the integrity of your IoT ecosystem.
