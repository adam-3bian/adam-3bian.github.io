# Wyvern Global

## Description

Wyvern Global Semiconductors is leading the way in memory-secure System on Chips (SoCs) with our Wyvern Advanced RISC-V Processor, known as WARP.

We are dedicated to providing the first commercially available SoCs in the world that are CHERI-native, which means they are built from the ground up with CHERI technology integrated. Our WARP SoCs are designed in-house for both BSD and [Real-Time Operating Systems](/software/real-time-operating-systems/) (RTOS). We design these for generic use in partner developer boards or for real OEM control system applications, offering end-to-end support for our clients.

We are currently developing a distribution center off Junction 34 of the M6, which will support our commercial operations and facilitate the distribution of these SoCs.

## CHERI Activities

We are currently developing WARP in-house and have received interest from numerous control system engineers across various industries. We are actively reaching out to these engineers to explore future partnership opportunities.

Additionally, we are in the process of our Series A funding round, aiming to raise £10 million. This funding will help us build our distribution center and support our operations as we establish our customer base. Furthermore, we have applied for a £4.8 million [UKRI](/organisations/ukri/) contract to procure these devices.

Our WARP architecture and toolchain will be available for modification and use, serving as a launchpad for designing custom OEM devices that integrate CHERI into their core systems. This includes everything from ECUs to mobile devices, as well as AI clusters and edge computing solutions. We will offer MPW access to procure these OEM versions of our WARP SoCs for a practicable cost, and walk our clients through the MPW process.

Our WARP 2532 System-on-Chips (SoCs) will provide a quick, general-purpose BSD-based solution for partner developer boards and small-scale industrial applications, creating an attractive entry point into the WARP ecosystem.
