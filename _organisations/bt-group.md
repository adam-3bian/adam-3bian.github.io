## Description
{: id="description" }

BT Group is the UK’s leading provider of fixed and mobile telecommunications and related secure digital products, solutions and services. We also provide managed telecommunications, security and network &amp; IT infrastructure services to customers across 180 countries.

## CHERI Activities
{: id="cheri-activities" }

BT Group manages a vast telecommunications infrastructure, and is an advocate of memory-safety technologies. Combining these factors, BT’s research and cybersecurity departments are exploring CHERI within a network services context and are keen to work with alliance partners to test and evaluate the technology.
