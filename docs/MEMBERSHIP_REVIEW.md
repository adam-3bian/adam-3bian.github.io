# Membership comparison — 20 October 2026

This is a read-only comparison against published CHERI Alliance member profiles. All 52 local member records are unchanged. The 21 founding-member badges use the existing structured status, with all 21 corroborated by accessible official profiles.

47 local records have an accessible corresponding official member profile. The member archive and member-type archive did not expose their dynamically populated roster to the browsing tool, so this is not an exhaustive certification of the live roster or a claim that there are no additional members. Five local profiles could not be independently rechecked through the available browsing tool; this does not establish that those organisations have left.

Findings requiring editorial review, deliberately not applied during this design-only pass:

- [Hildebrand](https://cheri-alliance.org/member/hildebrand/) is listed as a Full Member on the official profile; the local record has no membership tier.
- [National Taiwan University](https://cheri-alliance.org/member/national-taiwan-university/) is identified more specifically as “Secure Systems Lab at National Taiwan University” on the official profile.
- The official Birmingham profile uses the fuller centre name; its community and founding status agree with the local record.
