# Procedural asset catalogue and verification scope

The implementation uses solid-colour mesh geometry. A generator's existence does not establish accurate placement or completeness at every real location. The cooked geometry measurements and their explicit failures are in [reports/geometry.json](reports/geometry.json); the reproducible command is `node --expose-gc --import tsx scripts/audit-geometry.ts`. The corresponding [placed asset inventory](reports/placed-assets.json) preserves tile, stable emitted ID, position and the metadata actually returned by `buildTile`, supplemented by actual procedural tree roots. Its source hashes identify the measured snapshot. Dynamic renderer instances and terrain/road/rail ribbon components are outside that inventory.

Factory roots carry generator version, ID, seed, inferred source/confidence, local support anchors and bounds. Their child components receive stable IDs relative to the root. Batched tile meshes preserve aggregate rendered geometry; the inventory provides the emitted placement metadata separately. Missing metadata and duplicate emitted IDs, if any, are disclosed by the report rather than inferred into a false per-instance source record.

The CPU audit builds the selected tile coverage and measures finite attributes/indices, actual material texture inputs, sampled road/ballast terrain intrusion, actual tree-root soil contact, rendered building body/plinth contact, derived platform clearance in both travel directions, platform furniture support against emitted top triangles, and repeated tile-boundary geometry. It does not establish independent 2 m geography, a whole-route 10 mm contact certificate, every asset's continuous intersection volume, human route recognition, GPU performance or a two-hour soak. Read its recorded coverage and failures before describing any acceptance criterion as passed.

Cross-owner building footprint probes use the actual neighboring tile's cooked terrain through a precomputed coordinate cache. Road checks retain raw path-height warnings separately and match actual asphalt/footway triangles within 100 mm of the expected crown/profile, so an upper stacked deck cannot clear a lower-road intrusion. A 2 mm geometric edge allowance handles float32 quantization below the 10 mm contact threshold. Waiting-room support uses its declared levelling-plinth point and verifies the actual rendered plinth bounds. The report marks measured mandatory failures explicitly and returns a failing process status if they remain or source files change during the audit. Omitted calibration tiles and unmatched actual surfaces remain disclosed gaps.

The gauge probe intersects actual running rail-head triangles with cant-plane cross-sections at source-segment midpoints; switch spans are excluded for their dedicated trackwork checks. Ballast probes match actual canted-bed top triangles, including renderer-owned operative-switch beds, rather than comparing against a flat nominal height. Building retention is checked against a persisted baseline and the previously failing boundary cohort. [SOURCE-GAPS.md](SOURCE-GAPS.md), the [original rejection register](reports/authored-source-rejections.json) and its [supplement](reports/additional-source-rejections.json) record six omitted baseline buildings and their unresolved support/access rules. Their diagnostic records do not count as rendered buildings.

The final run built all 121 tiles and covered all 22 station regions without a hard or sampled mandatory failure. It measured 11,574 actual inner-face gauges, resolved all 7,035 cross-owner building queries and found no gap in the 22-building regression cohort. These measured results coexist with 574 unmatched same-level road probes, 187 rejected building-instance records and 843 unsupported cable-attachment candidates. Those coverage and source-fidelity gaps remain unaccepted.

The thirteen declared LOD0 factory probes in [asset-budgets.json](reports/asset-budgets.json) meet their per-unit triangle targets: the ordinary house has 576 triangles, the tree 92, road vehicles 412–636 and individual external rail cars 664–832. Reproduce with `node --import tsx scripts/asset-budgets.ts`. These parameter-specific probes do not certify every source footprint, LOD transitions or GPU performance.
