# Outstanding scenery and route work

**Acceptance remains incomplete.** The user reports **scenery sometimes failing to load**, incorrectly oriented level-crossing equipment at **Blakedown**, possibly at other crossings, and glitches with **road bridges and scenery beside the track**. Blakedown is a named reproduction target; the affected components and other sites still need front-view inspection. The latest reconstruction fixes the top bar, resizing, Hagley ground-strip/access/arch and lost source road bends. Current 121-tile geometry and 44-view browser evidence are recorded in [HANDOVER.md](../HANDOVER.md); the route remains unaccepted.

A subsequent partial fix retries failed tile fetches and worker packets up to four attempts, including while stationary; the latest loader also bounds requests, tags jobs, times out stalled fetches/builds and restarts a failed worker. Focused regressions are in [scenery-retry.test.ts](../tests/scenery-retry.test.ts). A software-browser fault injection exercises replacement and continued loading. This does not establish continuous loading coverage or complete the streaming gate.

The governing construction rule is: **reconcile the transport profiles, structures and required clearances first; then make terrain yield to them through cutting, filling, grading, retaining geometry and changes to the terrain mesh.** Roads, railway formations and structural openings must remain clear and supported. Terrain mesh topology is distinct from road/rail network connectivity. A ground edit cannot turn an overpass into a junction or move a correct railway to hide an intersection.

OpenTTD, OpenLoco and especially Transport Fever 2 are design references for consistent construction, connected networks, layers, support and automatic terrain adaptation. This does not require copying their algorithms, adopting a square-grid railway or adding a world-building interface.

## Required shared construction rules

These are requirements for the next implementation, **not a claim that a complete executable registry exists already**. Create the registry and `docs/WORLD-RULES.md`, with typed inputs, owning surfaces, permitted intersections, numeric limits, failure diagnostics and coverage for each rule. Apply them to every asset family and site, not just the reported examples.

| Rule | Invariant | Required validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TERRAIN-01 | Terrain yields to reconciled road/rail formations and reserved openings across their full footprint. | Test actual emitted terrain/transport triangles and void volumes, including curves, portals, junctions and LODs; require zero unintended intrusion. |
| LAYER-01 | Separate decks, ground, water, bores and underpasses retain their own levels, owners and connectivity. | Test stacked sites; support queries must select the intended owner/layer rather than the highest or nearest surface. Plan-view overlap cannot create a graph connection. |
| SUPPORT-01 | Every required anchor/footprint contacts its declared parent support; intentional burial is explicit. | Measure all required anchors and complete footing contact against final surfaces. Designed contacts/joins agree within 10 mm in model space; that is not permission for terrain to protrude through finished surfaces. Unmeasured anchors remain open. |
| JOIN-01 | Connected roads, rail beds, decks, ramps, stairs and platforms share compatible boundaries and profiles. | Check position, tangent, width/gauge, grade/cant, thickness and actual traversable surfaces; no floating approaches, gaps or abrupt incompatible transitions. |
| ORIENT-01 | Orientation follows the component's real host/use: rail tangent, road approach, building footprint or gravity. | Verify world-space component axes; include rail-aligned crossing grooves, road-facing heads and barriers across the appropriate lanes. Rotate/skew synthetic sites and reverse source polyline parameterisation without changing the equivalent physical site. |
| CLEARANCE-01 | Static structures and moving components stay outside incompatible swept volumes. | Declare site/stock-specific limits and measure minimum separation for full trains, road vehicles, pedestrians and barrier sweeps, including curvature/cant and bridge soffits. |
| STATE-01 | Rendered geometry, collision/navigation and road/rail control use the same solved site and authoritative state. | Check point blades/routes, signals, AWS/TPWS, doors and crossing states through movement, pause, save/load and offscreen operation. |
| REGEN-01 | Source/geometry changes rebuild every affected dependant; chunk order and LOD preserve the solved interfaces and operating state. | Test reversed build order, eviction/revisit, settings changes and source-profile edits. Reject stale cache entries and incomplete assemblies; keep failures and omissions in the register. |
| STREAM-01 | Required visible/near-field scenery is ready before use; failures and pending jobs have explicit recoverable states and cannot silently leave holes. | Measure required/attached tile IDs and deadlines during a continuous drive, including throttling, failed/stalled jobs, stationary retry, direction changes and eviction. Test finite loading/error behaviour without losing simulation state. |
| REFERENCE-01 | Accuracy claims refer to inspected images at supported matching locations/directions, with coverage and uncertainty disclosed. | Sample source time at five seconds (ten for quick QA), validate the alignment manifest, inspect paired front views and record expected/observed structural features. Keep unmapped/unreviewed/not-comparable cases explicit; image colour similarity alone cannot pass the rule. |
