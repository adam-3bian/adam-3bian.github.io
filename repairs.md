## Reporting a general repair

General repairs should be reported as soon as possible. Every member receives a repair book at the beginning of their tenancy. Complete a slip with a short description of the issue and submit it to the office at **15 Rochdale Walk**.

You can also report a repair by phone on **[0121 773 3251](tel:01217733251)**.

If your call cannot be answered, a voicemail service is available. Repairs are then dealt with by our administration team during office hours.

We aim to address and complete repairs as soon as possible, maintain properties to a high standard and prevent unnecessary disruption to members. Repairs that require estimates, specialist work, inspections or scaffolding may take longer.

### Examples of general repairs

- plasterwork repairs;
- carpentry repairs that are not hazardous;
- minor plumbing repairs that are not hazardous or causing uncontrollable damage;
- minor electrical issues that are not hazardous;
- door or window repairs that do not put the security of the property at risk;
- fence repairs that are not hazardous;
- repairs to floors and paths that are not hazardous.

Some repairs may require pre-work inspections, quotations or specialist equipment. This can mean they take longer to resolve. For questions about general repairs, contact the office during working hours.

## Emergency repairs

Emergency repairs are those that put the health, safety or security of members or others at immediate risk, or affect the structure of the building.

Examples include:

- gas leaks;
- total loss of electricity;
- unsafe electrical sockets or fittings;
- severe roof leaks;
- blockage of your only toilet;
- a burst water pipe where the supply cannot be switched off;
- minor water leaks that cannot be contained and may cause further property damage;
- securing a ground-floor external door or window;
- structural instability that is likely to cause harm;
- loss of keys, although members may be liable for the cost of replacement keys.

**Emergency-only contact number: [07856 487690](tel:07856487690).**

This number must only be used for genuine emergencies and not for general repairs or general enquiries. The source information also identifies **29 Rochdale Walk** as an out-of-hours emergency-only contact point.

### Gas, electricity and water emergencies

If you smell gas, turn off the gas at the mains supply if it is safe to do so, telephone **Cadent on [0800 111 999](tel:0800111999)** immediately, and inform the co-operative as soon as possible afterwards.

For an electricity or water emergency, turn off the relevant electricity or water supply if it is safe to do so.

## Contractor call-outs and missed appointments

We will update you on the status of a reported repair as soon as we are able. The administration team, or in some cases an approved third-party contractor, will contact you to arrange a visit.

Members may be liable for the cost of a call-out where an appointment is missed without reasonable notice to the contractor or administration team. Members may also be liable for the cost of an emergency call-out where the reported issue is not an emergency.
