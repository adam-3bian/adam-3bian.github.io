# HERI Research Working Group

The CHERI Research Working Group connects researchers in academia and industry, providing a forum to identify shared interests and collaborate.

## Areas of collaboration

- Research questions and possible solutions
- Evaluation methods, benchmarks, and analysis techniques
- Tools for hardware and software prototypes
- Research results, publications, and feedback
