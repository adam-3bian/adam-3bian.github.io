# CHERI Trademark

CHERI® is an open technology, but the CHERI name and logos are protected trademarks.

CHERI® is an open technology, but there are conditions to use the CHERI brand. These apply to all.

Protecting the CHERI brand helps ensure that when users see CHERI referenced in a product, service, membership mark, or certification claim, they can have confidence that the claim is genuine and accurately represented.

If you reference CHERI, use CHERI branding, claim CHERI compatibility, or describe products, services, membership, or certification in relation to CHERI, please review the policies and guidelines on this page.

## Trademark Policy

The [CHERI Alliance](/organisations/cheri-alliance/) Trademark Policy explains how the CHERI name, logos, membership marks, and certification marks may be used. It helps ensure that references to CHERI are accurate, fair, and not misleading.

The policy applies to everyone, including organisations that are not members of the Alliance. Some uses require prior permission, so please review the full policy before using CHERI trademarks or marks.
