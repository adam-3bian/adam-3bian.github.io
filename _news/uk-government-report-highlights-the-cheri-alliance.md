The Department for Science Innovation and Technology (DSIT) of the British Government has published [a report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cheri-adoption-and-diffusion-research/cheri-adoption-and-diffusion-research) about CHERI adoption and diffusion, to “understand the potential market” for CHERI and “how the Department can encourage and support the adoption and diffusion of the technology in semiconductors”.

We are proud to see that the CHERI Alliance is highlighted in this report, even though the Alliance has been very recently formed.
