# lowRISC and Partners to Deliver Commercial-Quality, Open-Source CHERI Secure Enclave with InnovateUK Support


[lowRISC CIC](/organisations/lowrisc/) and [Capabilities Limited](/organisations/capabilities-limited/), two founding members of the [CHERI Alliance](https://cheri-alliance.org/), have announced the launch of the [COSMIC Project](https://cosmic-project.lowrisc.org/), supported by the UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) and InnovateUK.

The 3 year initiative will deliver the first commercial-quality, open-source, CHERI-enabled 64-bit Secure Enclave for application-class system-on-chips (SoCs). By combining CHERI hardware memory safety and compartmentalisation with formal verification, COSMIC aims to create a secure foundation for future high-assurance silicon designs.

Built on OpenTitan® design principles and featuring the CVA6-CHERI processor from [Capabilities Limited](/organisations/capabilities-limited/), the project will extend open-source security to Linux-class systems and advance the commercial maturity of CHERI-enhanced RISC-V technology. Formal verification will ensure the design achieves a high standard of correctness suitable for deployment.

The [CHERI Alliance](https://cheri-alliance.org/) welcomes the announcement. COSMIC is an important step towards embedding CHERI-based security in next-generation commercial products.

COSMIC continues [lowRISC CIC](/organisations/lowrisc/)‘s mission to make commercial open-source silicon real. It follows the success of OpenTitan and Sunburst and is supported by partners including [Google](/organisations/capabilities-limited/), also a founding member of the CHERI Alliance, and Rivos.
