# 📣 Marketing WG

Coordinates accurate CHERI messaging, shared material, campaigns, events, and communication across the Alliance ecosystem.

CHERI needs explanations that are accurate, useful to different audiences, and clear about the maturity of the technology. The group coordinates shared messages and material so Alliance activity can be communicated consistently without flattening important technical distinctions.

Activity can include editorial planning, member stories, event promotion, campaign material, and review of public explanations. The group works alongside the Promotion & Ambassador Program while retaining a distinct focus on Alliance-wide communication.

Group type: Programme and ecosystem

## Areas of collaboration

- Shared messaging and material
- Campaigns and events
- Ecosystem communication
