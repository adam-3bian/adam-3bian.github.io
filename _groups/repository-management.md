# 💾 Repository Management WG

Develops consistent practices for the structure, maintenance, contribution, quality, and stewardship of shared Alliance repositories and technical collateral.

The Repository Management Working Group focuses on defining rules for participation, maintenance and contribution to CHERI open-source projects, managed or not by the Alliance. The repository management working group helps structure the CHERI-Alliance GitHub organisation.

Our aim is to have high quality resources available and to foster collaboration in the community. We also want to set up organisation-wide rules that improve the quality for our community.

Group type: Programme and ecosystem

Areas of collaboration: Repository structure and ownership

Contribution and maintenance
