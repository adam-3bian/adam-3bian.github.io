# 🪪 Promotion &amp; Ambassador Program WG
{: id="page-hero-title" .page-hero-title }

Helping the CHERI ecosystem communicate clearly, consistently, and effectively.
{: .page-hero-summary }

CHERI is relevant to a wide range of audiences, from developers and researchers to product teams, policymakers, educators, and security leaders. This group coordinates targeted outreach, coordination and community-engagement activities that help those audiences understand what CHERI is, where it is being used, and how it can support more secure computing.

The group supports promotion of the CHERI Alliance and development of the ambassador programme, helping expand awareness of CHERI across different regions, sectors, and communities.

## The Ambassador programme
{: id="the-ambassador-programme" }

The group manages and supports the [Ambassador programme](/alliance/leadership/ambassadors/), helping ambassadors share accurate information about CHERI within their professional, technical, academic, and regional networks.

  - Talks, demonstrations, and events
  - Community connections
