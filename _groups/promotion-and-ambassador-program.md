# 🪪 Promotion & Ambassador Program WG

Helping the CHERI ecosystem communicate clearly, consistently, and effectively.

CHERI is relevant to a wide range of audiences, from developers and researchers to product teams, policymakers, educators, and security leaders. This group coordinates targeted outreach, coordination and community-engagement activities that help those audiences understand what CHERI is, where it is being used, and how it can support more secure computing.

The group supports promotion of the [CHERI Alliance](/organisations/cheri-alliance/) and development of the ambassador programme, helping expand awareness of CHERI across different regions, sectors, and communities.

## The Ambassador programme

The group manages and supports the Ambassador programme, helping ambassadors share accurate information about CHERI within their professional, technical, academic, and regional networks.
