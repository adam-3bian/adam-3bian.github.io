Fire and gas safety are key issues for Small Heath Park Housing Co-operative.

## Fire safety

We urge all members to:

- regularly check that smoke alarms are operating as they should;
- think carefully about what they would do in the event of a fire;
- know their escape routes and ensure they are not blocked; and
- consider how they can help prevent fires.

## Gas safety

Small Heath Park Housing Co-operative is required to have its gas appliances inspected and maintained regularly, and to carry out gas safety checks when a new tenant moves in.

Members must give access to gas safety engineers when notified.

If you smell gas, follow the emergency information on the [repairs page]({{ '/repairs/' | relative_url }}).
