### CheriABI: Enforcing Valid Pointer Provenance and Minimizing Pointer Privilege in the POSIX C Run-time Environment

[Brooks Davis](/people/brooks-davis/), Robert N. M. Watson, Alexander Richardson, [Peter G. Neumann](/people/peter-g-neumann/), Simon W. Moore, [John Baldwin](/people/john-baldwin/), [David Chisnall](/people/david-chisnall/), [Jessica Clarke](/people/jessica-clarke/), [Nathaniel Wesley Filardo](/people/nathaniel-wesley-filardo/), [Khilan Gudka](/people/khilan-gudka/), [Alexandre Joannou](/people/alexandre-joannou/), and others. ACM ASPLOS 2019, 2019.

Presents the pure-capability CheriABI process environment and its treatment of pointer provenance and privilege.
