### Deprivileging Low-Level GPU Drivers Efficiently with User-Space Processes and CHERI Compartments

[Paul Metzger](/people/paul-metzger/), [A. Theodore Markettos](/people/a-theodore-markettos/), [Edward Tomasz Napierała](/people/edward-tomasz-napieraa/), [Matthew Naylor](/people/matthew-naylor/), Robert N. M. Watson, and [Timothy M. Jones](/people/timothy-m-jones/). ACM CCS 2025, 2025.

Evaluates process and CHERI-compartment designs for moving a performance-sensitive GPU driver out of the kernel.
