### CHERIoT RTOS: An OS for Fine-Grained Memory-Safe Compartments on Low-Cost Embedded Devices

[Saar Amar](/people/saar-amar/), [Tony Chen](/people/tony-chen/), [David Chisnall](/people/david-chisnall/), [Nathaniel Wesley Filardo](/people/nathaniel-wesley-filardo/), [Ben Laurie](/people/ben-laurie/), [Hugo Lefeuvre](/people/hugo-lefeuvre/), [Kunyan Liu](/people/kunyan-liu/), Simon W. Moore, [Robert Norton-Wright](/people/robert-norton-wright/), [Margo Seltzer](/people/margo-seltzer/), [Yucong Tao](/people/yucong-tao/), Robert N. M. Watson, and [Hongyan Xia](/people/hongyan-xia/). ACM SOSP 2025, 2025.

Describes the [CHERIoT](/hardware/cheriot/) RTOS and its fine-grained compartment model for resource-constrained embedded systems.
