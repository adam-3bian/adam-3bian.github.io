## Join the CHERI Alliance - Collaborate, Communicate, Be Seen

### What the Marketing Working Group Does

We help tell the story of CHERI: what it is, why it matters and how it’s already being used. We make the complex ideas behind CHERI easier to understand both inside and outside the tech world. That includes creating public-facing materials, supporting announcements, sharing real-world examples and helping people talk more confidently about secure by design.

### Working with Alliance Members

We work with you, not just for you. Whether you’re launching a product, publishing research, testing CHERI in practice or reflecting on lessons learned, we’re here to help you talk about it in the right way. Sometimes that means drafting a blog post or infographic. Sometimes it’s about giving your message a boost on social media or helping it reach the right audience.

### Why Communication Matters

Clear communication helps CHERI gain traction. It connects our work to funders, policy makers, collaborators and the public. For members, it means your efforts don’t stay hidden or isolated. You get to focus on doing the work and we help you share it with impact.

### Open to Ideas

If you have ideas, questions or requests we’re listening. Want to co-write something? Not sure how to talk about CHERI at an event? Just want to throw some thoughts around? [Get in touch](/alliance/membership/contact/).

### Be Part of It

[Join the CHERI Alliance](/alliance/membership/why-join/) and help shape the future of secure computing. It’s a chance to be part of a community that supports your work, shares your values and helps your ideas reach further.
